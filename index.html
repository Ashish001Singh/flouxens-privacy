Flouxens

Privacy Policy  ·  Effective date: May 19, 2026

Summary

Flouxens does not collect, upload, or share your financial data. All transaction data stays on your device. We do not have servers that store your SMS messages or bank transactions.

1. Who We Are

Flouxens is a personal finance app developed by Ashish Singh (ashish001.singh1997@gmail.com). The app automatically reads bank SMS messages to help you track expenses, categorise spending, and manage budgets — entirely on your device.

2. Data We Access

  • SMS messages — read-only access to incoming SMS. The app scans messages from known bank senders to extract transaction amounts, merchants, and dates. Raw SMS content is processed and stored locally on your device only. Nothing is uploaded to any server.
  • Notification access — optional, used only to show budget alerts locally on your device.
  • Internet access — used only for Play Billing to verify Pro subscription purchases. No financial data, SMS content, or personal information is transmitted over the internet.

3. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not upload your SMS messages to any server.
  • We do not store your bank account numbers, card numbers, or UPI IDs on our servers.
  • We do not sell or share your data with advertisers or third parties.
  • We do not track your location.
  • We do not collect analytics or crash reports in the current version.

4. AI Insights

AI-powered insights are not available in the current version of Flouxens. No spending data, SMS content, or any other information is transmitted to any AI service or external server. This feature may be introduced in a future release, at which point this policy will be updated accordingly.

5. Crash Reporting

Crash reporting is not active in the current version of Flouxens. No crash data, device information, or error traces are collected or transmitted.

6. Data Storage & Security

All your transaction data, SMS logs, and settings are stored locally on your device using Android's Room database. Data is sandboxed to the app and cannot be accessed by other apps. We recommend enabling Android device encryption for additional security.

7. Data Deletion

Uninstalling Flouxens permanently deletes all locally stored data. Within the app, you can also go to Settings → Data Management → Delete All Data to wipe all transactions and categories without uninstalling.

8. Children's Privacy

Flouxens is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. The effective date at the top will reflect the latest version. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
